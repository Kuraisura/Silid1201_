                         💡 TIPS & TRICKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FLASHLIGHT TOO BRIGHT?
→ Lower "Intensity" in Inspector (try 5-8)

FLASHLIGHT STILL NOT VISIBLE?
→ Make sure you're in a dark area
→ Check the light is attached to Camera (Hierarchy → Camera → Flashlight)

CROUCH TOO SLOW/FAST?
→ Adjust "Crouch Transition Speed" (higher = faster)

CROUCH NOT LOW ENOUGH?
→ Increase "Crouch Camera Height" (try 0.7 or 1.0)

SPRINT TOO FAST/SLOW?
→ Adjust "Run Speed" in Inspector
